# Series Concept: **“Give Me Time”**

A series of 30–60 second animated shorts with no spoken dialogue.

The two main characters are:

* **The Pappice**, tiny, hyperactive, and relentlessly determined.
* **The Walnut**, enormous compared to him, tough, impassive, and subtly provocative.

In every episode, the Pappice tries a new method to pierce the Walnut. The Walnut never speaks and barely moves, but reacts through its eyes, eyebrows, body tilts, tiny jolts, and subtle expressions of superiority.

The concept is inspired by the Neapolitan proverb:

**“The pappice says to the walnut: give me time, and I’ll pierce you.”**

## Sound Language

The Pappice never uses words. Instead, he produces a kind of **expressive buzzing**, capable of communicating intentions and emotions.

For example:

* a fast, rising buzz: excitement;
* a low, intermittent buzz: concentration;
* a high-pitched buzz: pain or surprise;
* a broken, uneven buzz: embarrassment;
* a long, threatening buzz: challenge;
* a short, satisfied “bzz-bzz!”: a brilliant idea;
* a buzz slowly fading out: temporary defeat.

The buzzing should never sound like a real language. Its meaning comes entirely from tone, rhythm, and body language.

The Walnut remains mostly silent. It only produces physical sounds such as:

* creaks;
* dull knocks;
* rolling sounds;
* vibrations;
* squeaks;
* a very rare “crack,” which becomes crucial to the story.

The comedy is driven by music, amplified sound effects, pauses, editing rhythm, and visual reactions.

# Recurring Episode Structure

Each episode follows a simple pattern:

1. The Pappice studies the Walnut.
2. He notices something or gets an idea.
3. He makes a short, excited buzz.
4. He prepares an elaborate plan.
5. The Walnut reacts with skepticism.
6. The plan fails spectacularly.
7. The Pappice gets back up and starts again.
8. In the final shot, a tiny new mark appears on the Walnut.

The Pappice loses almost every episode, but slowly advances in the larger battle.

# Ten Dialogue-Free Episodes

## 1. The Drill

The Pappice drags in a huge power drill.

He presses it against the Walnut, wipes the sweat from his forehead, and makes a small, satisfied buzz.

The Walnut slowly raises one eyebrow.

The Pappice switches on the drill.

The vibration shakes him violently. First his legs tremble, then his entire body. The drill begins dragging him in circles around the Walnut.

The Pappice is launched into the air and lands inside a cup of coffee.

Silence.

He reappears over the rim, completely soaked, and lets out an irritated buzz.

The Walnut seems to smile.

Then the Pappice notices a tiny scratch on its surface. His buzzing instantly changes into pure pride.

## 2. The Run-Up

The Pappice measures a long distance between himself and the Walnut.

He places a tiny starting line, crouches like a sprinter, and produces a low, focused buzz.

He takes off.

He runs faster and faster as the music builds like an epic action scene.

The Walnut remains perfectly still.

The Pappice closes his eyes a split second before impact.

Sudden silence.

He smashes into the shell and remains stuck to it for a few seconds, completely flattened.

Then he slowly slides to the ground while producing a weak, descending buzz.

The Walnut tilts slightly, as though taking a closer look at him.

The Pappice opens one eye, pops back into shape, and prepares for another run.

## 3. The Jackhammer

The Pappice arrives wearing a construction helmet and carrying a jackhammer.

He places tiny safety cones around the Walnut.

He studies a completely incomprehensible technical blueprint, nods confidently, and starts drilling.

The jackhammer does not damage the Walnut. Instead, it drills through the table underneath it.

The Pappice disappears into the hole.

The Walnut remains suspended for one second.

Then it falls into the hole and crushes him.

A very long pause.

A tiny arm emerges from the hole and gives a thumbs-up.

A muffled but optimistic buzz can be heard from underneath.

## 4. Karate

The Pappice puts on a headband.

He stretches, meditates, and performs a series of spectacular martial arts poses.

The Walnut watches without reacting.

The Pappice concentrates all his energy into one tiny arm. His buzzing grows louder and more intense.

He strikes.

Nothing happens.

The Pappice freezes, trying to preserve his dignity.

Then his hand begins throbbing and swelling. He hops around in circles, producing an extremely high-pitched buzz.

During his dance of pain, he accidentally headbutts the Walnut.

A microscopic dent appears.

The Pappice immediately stops complaining and assumes the pose of a martial arts master.

## 5. The Workout

The Pappice studies the Walnut, then looks at his own skinny arms.

A training montage begins.

He lifts coffee beans, does push-ups on a biscuit, runs around a cup, and punches a sugar packet.

At the end of the montage, he appears with enormous muscles.

He approaches the Walnut and tries to lift it.

His muscles expand more and more.

The Walnut rises by a single millimeter.

The Pappice smiles triumphantly.

Then his muscles suddenly deflate with the sound of a leaking balloon.

The Walnut drops and crushes him.

A faint buzz emerges from underneath the shell, followed by a tiny whistle.

## 6. The Laser

The Pappice builds an extremely complicated machine using a magnifying lens, a flashlight, teaspoons, and electrical wires.

He lowers a pair of safety goggles and activates the device.

A laser beam strikes the Walnut.

The Walnut does not react.

The beam bounces off the shell, reflects between several mirrors, and returns toward the Pappice.

He ducks just in time.

The laser slices his helmet perfectly in half.

The Pappice watches the two pieces fall to the ground.

Slowly, he looks back at the Walnut.

The Walnut appears to be holding back laughter.

The Pappice checks the point of impact: a small black burn mark remains.

He produces a victorious buzz and strikes a heroic pose.

Behind him, the machine catches fire.

## 7. Hypnosis

The Pappice builds a tiny pendulum using a thread and a seed.

He swings it slowly in front of the Walnut’s eyes.

He produces a low, repetitive buzz, as though trying to hypnotize it.

The Walnut’s eyes follow the pendulum.

The Pappice becomes excited and gestures for the shell to open.

Nothing happens.

He repeats the gesture more insistently.

Still nothing.

He keeps swinging the pendulum until he falls asleep himself.

The Walnut looks at the sleeping Pappice, then at the pendulum.

With a tiny movement, it blows the pendulum back toward him.

The seed hits the Pappice on the forehead and wakes him instantly.

Panicked buzzing.

## 8. The Disguise

The Pappice hides inside a fake shell painted to resemble a small walnut.

He slowly approaches the Walnut, trying to imitate the way it rolls.

The Walnut watches him suspiciously.

The Pappice stops and tries to remain perfectly still.

One of his legs accidentally sticks out of the disguise.

He pulls it back in.

Another leg appears.

He pulls that one back in too.

Then all his legs pop out at once.

The Walnut makes a tiny movement, and the disguise splits in two.

The Pappice is exposed. He smiles nervously and produces an innocent little buzz.

The Walnut starts rolling toward him.

The Pappice runs away.

## 9. The Army

The Pappice arrives alone and faces the Walnut.

He produces one short buzz.

Ten more Pappices appear.

Another buzz.

Fifty more arrive.

They all form a military formation.

For the first time, the Walnut looks slightly worried.

The army charges.

The Pappices climb over the shell, pull ropes, use pickaxes, and build scaffolding.

The Walnut tilts slightly.

Every Pappice falls like a row of dominoes.

Silence.

The original Pappice stands up, looks at the pile of fallen companions, and produces a short rallying buzz.

Every Pappice raises one arm at the same time.

They are still ready.

## 10. The First Crack

The Pappice arrives without tools.

The Walnut watches him, almost curious.

The Pappice sits in front of the same spot that has been hit, scratched, and burned in the previous episodes.

He takes a deep breath.

He begins gnawing.

The sound is tiny, regular, and almost insignificant:

**tick, tick, tick, tick.**

At first, the Walnut looks amused.

The Pappice continues.

Time passes. The light changes. A coffee cup is filled and emptied. Shadows move across the table.

The Pappice grows tired, but continues with his steady rhythm:

**bzz… tick… bzz… tick…**

Suddenly:

**CRACK.**

Complete silence.

The Pappice stops.

The Walnut opens its eyes wide.

A tiny crack has appeared in the shell.

The Pappice studies it, then slowly turns toward the Walnut.

He produces one small, satisfied buzz.

The Walnut swallows nervously.

Cut to black.

The title appears:

**GIVE ME TIME**

# Personality Through Movement

## The Pappice

He moves quickly, with exaggerated gestures and theatrical poses.

Even when he fails, he always tries to recover his dignity.

After every accident, he:

1. checks whether anyone saw him;
2. straightens himself up;
3. pretends everything went according to plan;
4. starts again.

## The Walnut

Its comedy comes from using as little movement as possible.

It can express itself through:

* one raised eyebrow;
* a sideways glance;
* a rotation of just a few degrees;
* a tiny smile;
* a slow blink;
* a slight movement backward when it begins to feel afraid.

The more frantic the Pappice becomes, the more still the Walnut should remain.

# Sound Identity

Every episode can begin with the same short audio motif:

1. three plucked notes;
2. the Pappice’s buzz;
3. one dull knock from the Walnut;
4. the title appears.

The ending can also follow a recurring ritual:

* the Pappice fails;
* a short silence;
* he discovers a tiny sign of progress;
* a satisfied buzz;
* musical cut.

The buzzing can become the series’ signature sound, recognizable even without dialogue.

# Possible Titles

* **Give Me Time**
* **Pappice vs. Walnut**
* **Buzz vs. Crack**
* **One Bite at a Time**
* **The Tough Nut**
* **Pappice’s Plan**
* **Slowly but Surely**

The strongest title remains **“Give Me Time”**, because it preserves the meaning of the original proverb without requiring any verbal explanation.
